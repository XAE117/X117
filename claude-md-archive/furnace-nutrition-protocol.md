# I. The Protocol

## OMAD — One Meal A Day (Daily Default)

**Eating Window:** 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM

**Fasting Window:** 5:00 PM – 3:00 PM next day (22 hours)

During your window, eat one substantial meal. This isn't restriction — it's consolidation. You're compressing your daily nutrition into a focused window so your body spends most of its time in a fasted, fat-burning state.

**Meal Composition Target:**

- Protein: 40-50% of plate (150g+ daily target) — chicken, fish, eggs, beef, Greek yogurt
- Healthy fats: 25-30% — avocado, olive oil, raw nuts (your existing snack swap)
- Complex carbs: 20-25% — sweet potato, rice, quinoa, vegetables
- Minimize: refined carbs, sugar, processed food, liquid calories
**The 218→200 Math:**

- 18 lbs to lose
- At 1.5 lbs/week = 12 weeks (target: ~April 29)
- At 2 lbs/week = 9 weeks (target: ~April 8)
- The 48-hour fasts accelerate this significantly
## Weekly 48-Hour Fast

**Schedule:** Sunday 5 PM → Tuesday 5 PM (or adjust based on your week)

This is your proven protocol. One full 48-hour fast per week creates a significant weekly caloric deficit and gives your system a deep reset.

**During the 48:**

- Water (unlimited)
- Black coffee (no cream, no sugar)
- Electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium — critical for avoiding headaches and fatigue)
- Herbal tea
- Nothing else
**Breaking the fast:** Break with something gentle — bone broth, eggs, or a small portion of your regular meal. Don't break with a massive feast. Give your digestion 30 minutes before your full meal.

## The Rolling 48s (Aspirational Target)

Once the weekly 48 feels effortless (typically 3-4 weeks in), you can experiment with rolling 48s: eat one OMAD meal, then fast 48 hours, eat one OMAD meal, fast 48 hours. This is the accelerated protocol. Don't force it — let the weekly 48s build your fasting muscle first.

# II. The Gabapentin Factor

Gabapentin increases appetite through GABA pathway effects and can alter carbohydrate metabolism. This is a pharmacological headwind — you're not weak, you're fighting chemistry.

**Action items:**

- Talk to prescriber about tapering or alternatives
- Until then, the OMAD structure is your best defense — it eliminates grazing opportunities that gabapentin cravings exploit
- During fasting windows, gabapentin hunger signals are just signals. You know from recovery: a craving is not a command

# IV. Tracking — The Data That Inspires

## Daily Weigh-In

Every morning, after bathroom, before water. Same scale, same spot. Log it.

The daily number will fluctuate — water retention, sodium, digestion. **Only the 7-day rolling average matters.** Daily weigh-ins feed the average; the average tells the truth.

## Daily Log Fields

Each day, either tell Claude or log somewhere quick:

- **Weight** (morning, post-bathroom)
- **Fasting status** (OMAD / Hour X of 48 / Broke fast at X)
- **Meal quality** (1-5 scale: 1 = junk, 5 = perfect macro balance)
- **Protein estimate** (rough grams — even "high/medium/low" works to start)
- **Cravings** (Y/N, and what triggered them if Y)
- **Exercise** (what you did, even if just a walk)
- **Energy** (1-5, ties into your existing Morning Inventory)

**Current capacity (low exercise, building):**

- On resistance training days, prioritize protein even more
- Time your OMAD meal for after your training session when possible
- Don't do heavy resistance training during your 48-hour fast — walks and Primer only

# VI. Red Flags & Adjustments

**Adjust the protocol if:**

- Energy drops below 2 for more than 3 consecutive days — may need to add a small protein snack during extended fasts
- Long COVID symptoms flare noticeably — fasting can occasionally trigger immune responses. Listen to your body
- Weight loss exceeds 3 lbs/week sustained — too aggressive, you're losing muscle
- Dizziness, heart palpitations, or fainting — electrolytes first, but see a doctor if persistent
**This is not a crash diet.** This is a structured protocol you've already proven works for your body. The system just makes it visible and accountable.

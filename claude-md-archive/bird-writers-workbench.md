## The 7 Steps to a Finished Episode 1 Script

- [ ] **Step 1:** Read the deep draft out loud, start to finish. Time it. Note what lands and what drags. Don't fix anything.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Mark the 3 strongest moments and the 1 weakest. Write one sentence about each.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Convert Cold Open to production format — scene headings, action lines, dialogue. This is FORMATTING, not writing. 30 minutes max.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Convert Scenes 1-3 (Kitchen, Study, Backyard). Same formatting pass. Don't rewrite. Translate.
- [ ] **Step 5:** Convert Scenes 4-6 (Bedroom, Forum Post, Photo Erased). You'll be in rhythm by now.
- [ ] **Step 6:** One dialogue pass — read only the words people say. Do they sound like people? McCarthy rules: no quotation marks.
- [ ] **Step 7:** One visual pass — camera directions, lighting notes, sound cues. The clicking. The thermos. The photo.
> ✅ **Episode 1 is DONE when:** You can read it aloud in 10-12 minutes and it makes you feel something. Not perfect. Not polished. Felt.

# The Rules (Tape These to the Wall)

1. **No more research.** The mythology codex is 1,000 lines. You have enough.
1. **No more outlining.** 200+ pages of development material exist. Write scenes.
1. **Formatting counts as writing.** Converting prose to script IS progress.
1. **Imperfect scenes > perfect plans.** Ship rough. Revise later.
1. **The tonal test:** Dark, funny, hopeful, bleak, and full of love — simultaneously.
1. **When stuck:** "What does the story want? Now why won't you let it have that?"
1. **Side doors are valid.** Write whatever scene has low resistance today.
